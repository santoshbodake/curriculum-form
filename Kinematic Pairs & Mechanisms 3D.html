⚙️ Kinematic Pairs & Mechanisms in 3D

Choose an item, drag the 3D view to orbit (scroll or pinch to zoom) and use the sliders to move parts. Tick 🔒 to lock an input.

Key concepts

Lower vs higher pair

Lower pairs touch over a surface (pin, slider, screw, sphere, plane). Higher pairs touch at a point or line (cam, gear teeth).

Constrained motion

Completely: one possible motion whatever the load. Incompletely: several motions possible. Successfully: a spring, weight or shoulder forces one path.

Mobility (Grübler)

F = 3(n − 1) − 2j₁ − j₂. 3-bar: F = 0 (rigid). 4-bar: F = 1. 5-bar: F = 2, so two inputs are needed, and locking one leaves F = 1.
